Bridal Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my bridal appointment? ▼

We recommend booking your bridal consultation at least 4 to 8 weeks before your wedding date to secure your preferred appointment date and allow ample time for pre-wedding trials and custom wig preparations.

Do you offer pre-wedding hair trial sessions? ▼

Yes! Pre-wedding hair trials are highly recommended. During the trial, we test different hair styles, veil placements, and accessory fittings to finalize your exact wedding day look.

Do you travel for destination or out-of-state weddings? ▼

Yes! Our lead stylists travel across Nigeria (Lagos, Abuja, Ogun, Osun, and beyond) and for international destination weddings. Travel and accommodation terms are arranged during consultation.

Can Bolbash style my bridal party as well? ▼

Absolutely. We offer complete bridal party packages for mother of the bride, maid of honor, and bridesmaids. Please mention your party size during booking so we allocate sufficient time and stylists.
